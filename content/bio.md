I am a first-year Master's student at **Tsinghua University** (2025–), pursuing an interdisciplinary degree in Medicine & Engineering under the supervision of Prof. Jiahong Dong, CAE Academician. My research focuses on **multimodal medical image processing**, with broader interests spanning micro-nano robotics, brain-computer interfaces, and AI-assisted diagnosis.

I completed my Bachelor of Clinical Medicine (GPA: 4.0/4.0, Rank: 1/82) at **Jilin University** in 2025. During my undergraduate years, I published **7 SCI/conference papers** (5 as first/co-first author), presented posters at **ASH 2023**, and gained international research experience at Westlake University and the University of Cambridge (supervised by Prof. Pietro Lio, Machine Learning & Computer Vision).

Beyond research, I am deeply involved in the entrepreneurship ecosystem at Tsinghua — serving as Chief Administrative Officer of the Medicine & Engineering Team, Vice President of the Student Entrepreneurship Association, and founding CEO of **MOOD**, an AI × neuroscience multimodal emotional regulation system supported by Tsinghua's iCenter.
